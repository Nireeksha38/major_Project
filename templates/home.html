Core System Features

Algorithmic modules integrated into the live detection pipeline

👁️

Person Detection

Fine-tuned YOLOv8 model paired with Gaussian Soft-NMS to accurately detect dense, overlapping pedestrians without bounding-box suppression.

🎯

Movement Tracking

Deep SORT tracking with Kalman filtering to maintain continuous individual IDs, trajectories, and instantaneous velocity estimations.

🌊

Flow & Entropy

Gunner Farneback dense optical flow and Shannon directional entropy calculation to identify chaotic multi-directional stampede panic waves.

⚠️

Fall & Trample Alert

Aspect-ratio geometry and sudden height drop velocity analysis to immediately flag fallen persons on the ground to prevent trampling.
