🔬 Scientific Role of Key Modules
  • YOLOv8: Fast spatial person detection in each video frame.
  • Soft-NMS: Prevents false person dropouts in overlapping dense crowds.
  • Deep SORT: Links detections across time into continuous person trajectories.
  • Optical Flow: Captures sub-pixel motion intensity between consecutive frames.
  • Motion Entropy: Mathematical disorder index. Unidirectional flow = low entropy; stampede scattering = high entropy.
  • Fall Detection: Analyzes rapid aspect ratio transformations ($w/h > 1.15$) and downward velocity drops.
📋 Limitations & Real-World Considerations
  • 📌 Camera Calibration: Physical people/m² and km/h require scene perspective homography calibration. Normalized units $[0.0, 1.0]$ are utilized without homography.
  • 📌 Camera Angle: Steep top-down camera views yield the highest tracking accuracy with minimal occlusion.
  • 📌 Illumination: Night scenes with poor lighting may require infrared sensors or fine-tuning on low-light datasets.
  • 📌 Temporal Window: Panic detection observes temporal persistence across 15 frames to prevent single-frame false triggers.
