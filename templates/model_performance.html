⚙️ How the Stampede Prediction System Operates Efficiently

The surveillance engine executes a tightly coupled 6-stage algorithmic pipeline designed for real-time edge performance without requiring high-end GPUs:

Stage 1 Anchor-Free YOLOv8 Feature Extraction

Frames are normalized to 384×384 (or 640×640) resolution and passed through CSPDarknet backbone with PAN-FPN feature pyramid layers to detect people across varying camera heights, angles, and distances.

Stage 2 Gaussian Soft-NMS Dense Overlap Preservation

Instead of aggressively dropping overlapping boxes with Greedy Hard-NMS, the continuous decay formula s_i = s_i · exp(-IoU² / σ) softly attenuates confidence, keeping overlapping pedestrians in tight crowds visible and counted.

Stage 3 Deep SORT Trajectory & Velocity Tracking

8-state Kalman filters predict bounding-box trajectories across consecutive frames. Mahalanobis distance association preserves continuous person IDs, calculating individual walking speeds and direction vectors.

Stage 4 Farneback Optical Flow & Kinetic Momentum

Polynomial expansion optical flow quantifies dense pixel displacement vectors across frames, computing real-time motion intensity and sudden forward kinetic surges.

Stage 5 Directional Shannon Entropy (Chaos Index)

Motion direction vectors are categorized into an 8-bin orientation histogram. Entropy is evaluated as H = -Σ p_i log₂(p_i). High entropy indicates chaotic panic scattering, while low entropy represents organized crowd movement.

Stage 6 Multi-Factor Risk Classification & Fall Guard

Fuses density (20%), speed (20%), motion intensity (20%), entropy (15%), panic dynamics (10%), and fall geometry (15%) into a normalized composite risk index [0.0 - 1.0], triggering alerts with cooldown throttling.
