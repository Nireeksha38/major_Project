🛠️ Camera Troubleshooting & Privacy Permissions Guide
Click to toggle tips ▼
1. Browser Camera Permission (Top Recommended)

When clicking Start Camera in Browser WebCam mode:

  • Click "Allow" when the browser prompts for camera access.
  • If blocked, click the 🔒 / 📷 icon in your browser URL bar and set Camera to Allow.
2. Background App Conflicts (Teams, Zoom, Meet)

Webcams can only be accessed by one application at a time:

  • Close Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Discord, or OBS.
  • Close any other browser tabs using the webcam.
3. Windows 10/11 Privacy Settings

To enable system-wide camera access:

  1. Press Win + I ➔ Privacy & Security ➔ Camera.
  2. Turn Camera Access ON.
  3. Ensure "Let desktop apps access your camera" is ON.
4. Multi-Camera & Physical Privacy Slider

Check if your laptop has a sliding camera privacy cover or a mute key (like Fn + F10). You can switch between your laptop built-in camera and external USB camera using the dropdown.
